# EcoLoop AI — 3-Minute Video Demo Script
## AI Builder Cup Sustainability Hackathon Public Demonstration

**Target Duration**: 2 minutes 50 seconds (under the 3-minute limit)  
**Presenter Tone**: Confident, mission-driven, technical yet accessible  
**Key Differentiator to Emphasize**: **Verified Recovery** — waste is only counted when physically weighed and accepted by authorized collectors.

---

### [00:00 – 00:25] Introduction & The Real Problem
- **Screen**: Camera on presenter, then cut to EcoLoop AI landing page hero banner.
- **Visual**: Show the EcoLoop AI logo and title: *"Turn Household Waste into Verified Value"*.
- **Spoken**:
  > *"Every year, millions of tons of recyclable waste end up in landfills because of two problems: residents can't tell recyclable plastics from multi-layer packaging, and ordinary green apps report waste as 'recycled' the second an AI classifies it.*
  > *This is EcoLoop AI. Built for the AI Builder Cup hackathon using Google Gemini 2.5 Flash and Google Cloud, EcoLoop AI is an AI-powered circular economy platform where environmental impact is grounded in **verified physical recovery**, not unchecked claims."*

---

### [00:25 – 01:05] Multimodal AI Scanning & Calibrated Uncertainty
- **Screen**: AI Scanner tab (`http://localhost:3000`).
- **Visual Action**: Click on the curated demo item **"Milma Milk Pouch (Empty)"**. Click **"Classify with Google Gemini AI"**. Show the live reasoning ticker.
- **Spoken**:
  > *"Let's test this with a classic Kerala household challenge: an empty Milma milk pouch. In seconds, Gemini 2.5 analyzes the image and identifies it not as generic plastic, but as Multi-layer Flexible Plastic (MLP).*
  > *Notice how the AI flags a Contamination Alert: sour milk residue inside the pouch must be cleaned. And look at this: it doesn't give a fake 100% confidence. It explains the material composition and cites the official Clean Kerala Company protocol: clean MLP packets are shredded to reinforce PWD asphalt road-tarring."*

---

### [01:05 – 01:30] Bilingual Kerala Guidance & Handover Request
- **Screen**: Click the **"മലയാളം"** language toggle at the top right.
- **Visual Action**: Show instant rendering of Malayalam instructions. Then click **"Schedule Handover Request"** to open the pre-filled booking modal and submit.
- **Spoken**:
  > *"With one click, the entire platform switches to Malayalam, ensuring grassroots accessibility for households across Kerala.*
  > *The resident sees their preparation checklist: wash with soap water, cut open, dry in the sun, and book a collection with Haritha Karma Sena Ward 14. We click 'Submit Request', and the item is queued."*

---

### [01:30 – 02:10] The Differentiator: Physical Collector Scale Verification
- **Screen**: Click on the **"Collector Portal"** tab.
- **Visual Action**: Show the pending queue. Select the newly submitted item. Show the scale reading input (3.5 kg) and contamination deduction (0.3 kg). Click **"Verify & Issue Receipt"**. Show the confetti explosion.
- **Spoken**:
  > *"Here is what makes EcoLoop AI radically different from greenwashing apps.*
  > *This is the Collector Portal used by Haritha Karma Sena leads and municipal MCF operators. The collector places the sack on a certified physical scale: 3.5 kilograms. They notice 300 grams of dirty caps, which are deducted.*
  > *When the collector clicks 'Verify & Issue Receipt', a tamper-evident digital certificate is generated, and ONLY NOW does this material count toward real landfill diversion!"*

---

### [02:10 – 02:35] Auditable Impact Dashboard & Methodology
- **Screen**: Click on the **"Impact Dashboard"** tab.
- **Visual Action**: Highlight the real-time updated KPIs: Landfill Diverted Mass (kg), Avoided GHG (kg CO₂e), Conserved Water, and the verified ledger.
- **Spoken**:
  > *"Instantly, our Sustainability Impact Ledger updates in real time. We see exactly 10.5 kilograms diverted, 46.6 kilograms of CO₂ equivalent avoided, and our freshwater and energy savings.*
  > *Below, every gram is traceable to a signed digital receipt and certified collector. Our emission factors strictly reference Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) India standards and the US EPA WARM model."*

---

### [02:35 – 02:50] Google Cloud Architecture & Closing Statement
- **Screen**: Split view showing the deployed application and the Google Cloud Run architecture diagram.
- **Spoken**:
  > *"EcoLoop AI runs as a containerized Next.js fullstack service on Google Cloud Run, powered by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash and Cloud Firestore.*
  > *By bridging household smartphone cameras with grassroots sanitation workers, EcoLoop AI turns waste into verified circular value. Thank you!"*
